ShellWhy

Paste a command line. See where bash cuts it into words and which quotes it removes, before any variable, glob or command is expanded.

plain'single'"double"\ backslash$'ANSI-C'$expansion
Checked against real bash 5.1.16: 346,000 generated quoting-only command lines (plain text, single, double and backslash quoting, $'...'), engine and bash agreed on every one (0 mismatches). Operators, comments and expansions ($var, $(...), globs, braces, ~) are shown and flagged, not evaluated, and were outside that test. The bash manual could not be fetched; POSIX 2.2 Quoting was read. The $'...' rules come from testing bash. Everything runs in your browser.
