What it does

  • Splits a command line into words and colours each piece by quoting kind: plain, single, double, backslash, $'...' and expansion.
  • Shows the argv the program receives, or flags what depends on your shell: variables, globs, braces, tilde, command substitution.
  • Reports unterminated quotes, and lists operators and comments.

How far to trust it

  • On 346,000 generated quoting-only lines, it agrees with real bash 5.1.16 on every one. Operators and expansions are shown, not evaluated, and were not part of that test. The bash manual could not be fetched; POSIX 2.2 Quoting was read.
